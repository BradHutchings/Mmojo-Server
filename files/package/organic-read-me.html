Thank You!

Thank you for trying Mmojo Organic! I am humbled that you're giving this a few minutes of your time.

Think of Mmojo Organic first as an Emergency Large Language Model (LLM). Use it when your favorite cloud LLM isn't working, your Internet is down, or you need a chat to stay local and private.

When you're comfortable with Mmojo Organic, make a folder on your desktop and drag the contents of the Mmojo Organic disk into that folder. You can run Mmojo Organic without the disk and without any complicated installation. It's still totally private. No chat data is ever sent to he cloud or saved to your disk by Mmojo Organic.

I developed Mmojo Organic because I was tired of Big AI lying to you. You can use LLMs privately on the computer you have without divulging your personal or corporate secrets to these companies. I would love to know how you're using Mmojo Organic! Drop me an email.
